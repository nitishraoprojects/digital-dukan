Questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?

Our websites start at ₹2,000 for a single-page Starter site, ₹4,999 for a Business website and ₹9,999 for a Premium experience. The final price depends on your pages, features, content and complexity — we give you a clear written quote after a free consultation.

Can you build a website for my small shop?

Absolutely. Small shops are exactly who we built Nikavi Studio for. Even a simple one-page site with your products, timings, location and a WhatsApp button can bring you new customers.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the scope and how quickly we receive your content. A Starter site is typically ready within a few days; larger multi-page or custom websites take longer. We'll share a realistic timeline in your quote.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website we build is designed mobile-first and tested across phones, tablets and desktops — because most of your customers will find you on their phone.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. We can set up your site so common updates are simple, or handle updates for you through a maintenance plan. You're never locked in.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

We can help you choose, purchase and set up a domain and hosting, or work with what you already have. Domain and hosting costs are billed separately at actual cost from the provider.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Start with our free website audit — we'll show you what's holding your current site back, then propose a redesign that fixes it.

Will my website appear on Google?

We build every site with SEO-friendly foundations — clean structure, metadata, speed and mobile-friendliness — and can help set up Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile. Rankings depend on many factors over time, so we won't promise a position, but we'll give your site the right start.

Do you provide maintenance?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance and support for updates, fixes, backups and content changes. Maintenance is priced separately based on what you need.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just a conversation. Tell us about your business, customers and goals. After that, we'll need your logo (if you have one), photos, service or product details, and contact information — and we'll guide you through the rest.
